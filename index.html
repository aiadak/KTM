System Map

Five steps, one compounding loop: the outgoing SME's judgment is swept in during Planning, drawn out during KT, hardened through Shadow and Reverse Shadow, and finally turned into automation and agent-ready work that feeds new opportunities back into the platform.

Step 1 · Planning (Pre-KT)
Archivist · Interview Planner
Sweep the uploaded corpus; live Jira only on request. Turn the gaps into a per-application session plan.
↓
Step 2 · Knowledge Transition
Observer · Interviewer · Extractor · Playbook Composer
Capture the sessions; extract rules and heuristics; compose the living Playbook.
↓
Step 3 · Shadow
Knowledge Graph · Reviewer Copilot (HITL)
Wire the causal graph; the SME's approve / edit / reject diff drives the confidence score.
↓
Step 4 · Reverse Shadow
Completeness Validator · Coach · Knowledge Copilot
Prove coverage; coach the incoming team; assist live while they run solo.
↓
Step 5 · Compounding Intelligence
Automation Prioritizer
Score every procedure for automation; surface reactive→proactive and agent-ready work — the loop that lets the team run with a lighter footprint.
